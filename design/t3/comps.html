Dark — the night planner

World

A UK office wall planner: printed ink on laminated stock, a hairline grid, weekend columns shaded. Nothing is coloured until you do something; each tick presses a flat dot sticker onto the day. Misses stay blank, never red.

Palette

Two values per scheme plus four sticker colours. Light: stock #F7F9F8, ink #14324F. Dark: ground #0E1C2B, chalk #E6EEF2. Every neutral is a fixed tint of the ink: secondary text 72 % (5.3:1 light), grid 16 %, weekend shade 7 %. Stickers, light / dark: Calendar #2A6DB8 / #6AA5EC, Habits #3E9C55 / #5DBB72, To-dos #E8B92F / #F0C94A, Steps #E0573E / #F07A5F. Stickers mean source or achievement only. Every action (Grant, Save, FAB, Undo) is in ink.

Type

Barlow Condensed (500/600/700) for the masthead, section heads, times and every numeral, tabular. Barlow (400/500/600) for names, titles and body. Both OFL, bundled as res/font in T4 (no downloadable-fonts provider). All sizes in sp.

Structure

No cards and no elevation. The dashboard is one ruled sheet: a 2 dp ink rule opens each section, 1 dp grid lines separate rows, 4 dp corners throughout (8 dp on the FAB, 12 dp on the sheet's top). Each section head is a 48 dp target that opens its screen (Calendar and Steps open Settings).

Calendar

All-day events as washed bars. The next event, or the one in progress, is set large with its end time and a Now stamp. Every timed event carries a duration rule whose length is proportional to its length (full width = 2 h, capped); the tick on the in-progress rule marks now.

Habits

Left: today's tick, 48 dp. Middle: the name and this week's chain, Mon–Sun under one shared letter row, today underlined. A green dot is a done day, an empty ring a missed scheduled day, a shaded square an unscheduled day (the planner's weekend tint), a dotted ring a day still to come. Right: the streak, large. Signature moment: the tick presses the sticker on, 1.12 → 1 scale with a 3° settle over 200 ms; instant when animations are off.

To-dos and Steps

To-dos on ruled lines. Overdue is marked in heavy ink caps with a small yellow pennant, not red. Done-today items keep a yellow tick and strike through. Steps: a flat ring (today / goal) beside the uncapped count, then 7 bars, today last and outlined. Bars that met the goal turn tomato; the rest stay in ink tint. Colour is earned.

Order

D18 kept: Calendar → Habits → To-dos → Steps. The planner reads time first, then what you do with it. On a 412 dp phone, Calendar and the Habits head land above the fold; at 360 dp, Calendar holds the first viewport.
